Podcast Reflection: My 1st Podcast Experience

Did you know? Recently, I just started a podcast called Safe Browsing, which is about data privacy and security. The 1st episode was about… well… how to protect data privacy, duh. You can listen to the podcast here:



I chose this topic since I care a lot about data privacy and security, and I wanna use podcast as a medium to raise others’ awareness on them.
Screenshots of Privacy Tools that I Use and Recommend (left: Mullvad VPN; Right: LibreWolf Browser)


How the podcast was made
Firstly, I wrote the script for the podcast and tried to record 5 different audio clips for each sessions of the podcast with the recording script that I created a while ago to see if I needed to cut any contents or not. Thankfully I didn’t need to as the timing of the podcast was perfect (exactly 5 minutes), but I had to record my voice clips multiple times as I wasn’t happy with some of them. Next, I merged the audio clips using Audacity, a free and open source audio editor. I also added some musics from an OST (original soundtrack) of a game I like to make the podcast feel less dull (lot of online videos include video game soundtracks as background musics as they’re usually good for noncommercial use since people won’t be able to upload videos about the game otherwise).


Final verdict: what I learned and how can I improve
Since I’m already quite experienced regarding data privacy, and I also have had some experiences with Audacity (I'd used it to edit audios before), I didn’t learn much about them (but that also means it took me less time to make this podcast episode). Still, I did learn more about how to write a persuasive script and improve my pronunciation, so it wasn’t all for nothing (plus it was fun!)

For future podcast episodes, or just contents that need to include my voice in general, I think I might try to sound a little bit more enthusiastic… Well… just a little… (I know my voice sounds boring but I can’t change that that’s just how I sound -_-) I also need to work on my pronunciation more (even tho I just said it got improved) as it still doesn’t sound good enough for me. I should also include more sources to make the podcast more persuasive. But overall, I think I’m quite happy with how the podcast turns out, and it was an enjoyable experience for me. I’m looking forward to create more contents on digital literacy!
